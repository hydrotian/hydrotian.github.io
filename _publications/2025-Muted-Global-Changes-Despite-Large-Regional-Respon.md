Wetlands play a crucial role in the global carbon, water, and energy cycles, yet their response to future climate change remains highly uncertain due to varying surface and subsurface hydrological processes. Here, we simulate wetland dynamics using a global land-river coupled model with improved wetland hydrology scheme.
